Information-Theoretic Quantification of Tropical Convective Organization

An entropy-based view of convective structure.

Develops an entropy-based Hierarchical Organization Index (HOI) and a dual-metric framework integrating size hierarchy and spatial clustering to quantify convective organization.

Identifies an adaptive structure-optimal infrared brightness temperature threshold and its long-term cooling trend, indicating systematic structural changes in tropical convective systems.
