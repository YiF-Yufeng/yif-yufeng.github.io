A Spatiotemporal Packing Framework for Tropical Cyclone Frequency

Dissertation

Proposes a framework showing that global tropical cyclone (TC) frequency emerges from the spatiotemporal packing and self-organization of the tropical climate system under finite environmental constraints.

This framework links spatial convective organization, genesis interval statistics, and TC lifecycle effects to explain the regulation of TC frequency.
